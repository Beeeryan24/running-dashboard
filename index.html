Strava sync (pull runs straight from your Strava account)
Get a token with permission to read activities (one time)
  1. Click 1. Authorize access, approve the Strava screen, then copy the code= value from the address bar of the page that fails to load.
  2. Paste it below with your client secret and click Exchange. They go only to Strava from your browser and are not saved.

The token is kept only in this browser. Never put your client secret or token inside the page's code or on GitHub. Synced runs are merged with your existing data and skip runs already on the dashboard (same day, similar distance). Strava cadence is per leg, so it is doubled.
